OVERLOAD — fitness & nutrition app prototype
=============================================

WHAT THIS IS
  A working frontend prototype of the Overload app. It runs entirely in a
  web browser — no install, no backend, no internet required for the core.

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click "overload.html". It opens in your default browser.
  On the first screen, either enter your own details or tap
  "Explore with sample data" to see it fully populated.

WHAT WORKS (all offline, in your browser)
  - Today dashboard: calories eaten vs burned, the day's deficit, protein.
  - Compete: friends step leaderboard, streaks, badges, Enigma Gym vs a
    rival gym, nudge & cheer, weekly reset with a crowned winner.
  - Food: search a West-African food database, log manually, or add custom.
  - Check-in: describe your day in plain language (AI feature, see below).
  - Learn: a Training course and a Mindset course, calisthenics ladders,
    and a progressive-overload planner.
  - Progress: weigh-ins, a weight-trend chart, and adaptive burn that
    learns your real numbers over time.

THE TWO AI FEATURES
  Photo calorie estimates and the conversational check-in call Claude
  directly, so they only work when the page is opened inside Claude.
  In the finished app these would run on your own server. Everything
  else works without them.

DATA
  Your entries are saved in the browser you open it with (local only).
  Nothing is uploaded. Clearing that browser's site data resets it.

STATUS
  Prototype / frontend only. The leaderboard friends and gym numbers are
  sample data; social features need a backend to become live and shared.

Built 1 October 2026.
